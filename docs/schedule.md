# Schedule

The course runs for **6 weeks**: 5 content sessions and one session for final presentations. Both sections cover exactly the same material each week.

| Week | Casa Central<br><small>Wednesday 16:05–18:40</small> | Vitacura<br><small>Friday 11:05–13:40</small> | Session | Sprint |
|:--:|:--:|:--:|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 28 | Oct 30 | [Python in Google Colab](#session-1-python-in-google-colab) | **Sprint 1** starts |
| 2 | Nov 4 | Nov 6 | [Data analysis with pandas](#session-2-data-analysis-with-pandas) | Sprint 1 review · **Sprint 2** starts |
| 3 | Nov 11 | Nov 13 | [Linear regression and model evaluation](#session-3-linear-regression-and-model-evaluation) | Sprint 2 review · **Sprint 3** starts |
| 4 | Nov 18 | Nov 20 | [Classification: logistic regression and metrics](#session-4-classification-logistic-regression-and-metrics) | Modeling clinic |
| 5 | Nov 25 | Nov 27 | [Trees, random forests, and communicating results](#session-5-trees-random-forests-and-communicating-results) | Sprint 3 review |
| 6 | **Dec 2** | **Dec 4** | [Final presentations](#session-6-final-presentations) | Final assessment |

!!! info "Key project dates"
    - **Team and case registration:** at the end of session 1.
    - **Notebook and slides due:** Tuesday, **December 1**, 23:59 (both sections).
    - **Presentations:** during each section's class time, **December 2** (Casa Central) and **December 4** (Vitacura).

Each session lasts 2 hours and 35 minutes and has two parts: a **first block** of concepts with live coding and, after a break, a **workshop** where teams apply what they just learned to their project.

---

## Session 1 · Python in Google Colab

*Casa Central: Wednesday, October 28 · Vitacura: Friday, October 30*

**By the end you can:** open and run notebooks in Colab; work with variables, data types, lists, dictionaries, conditionals, and functions; and operate on NumPy arrays.

- [Colab guide](colab.md): open, save a copy, run, and share.
- [Class 1: Introduction to Python](classes/Class_1_Intro_to_Python.ipynb)
- [Class 2: NumPy](classes/Class_2_NumPy.ipynb)

**Workshop:** form teams, choose a case, and load the data into a shared notebook. → [Sprint 1](project/sprints.md#sprint-1-data-in-colab)

---

## Session 2 · Data analysis with pandas

*Casa Central: Wednesday, November 4 · Vitacura: Friday, November 6*

**By the end you can:** read CSV and Excel files; select, filter, group, and sort data; spot missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent categories; and build exploratory charts with seaborn.

- [Class 3: pandas](classes/Class_3_Pandas.ipynb)
- [Supplement: visualization with seaborn](classes/Supplement_Seaborn.ipynb)

**Workshop:** Sprint 1 review; cleaning and exploratory analysis of your case. → [Sprint 2](project/sprints.md#sprint-2-data-cleaning-and-exploratory-analysis)

---

## Session 3 · Linear regression and model evaluation

*Casa Central: Wednesday, November 11 · Vitacura: Friday, November 13*

**By the end you can:** split data into training and test sets, encode categorical variables, train a linear regression, interpret its coefficients, measure error (MAE, RMSE, R²), and recognize overfitting.

- [Class 6: Linear regression](classes/Class_6_Linear_Regression.ipynb)
- [Class 6a: Underfitting and overfitting](classes/Class_6a_Underfitting_and_Overfitting.ipynb)
- [Supplement: cross-validation](classes/Class_7_Cross_Validation.ipynb)

**Workshop:** Sprint 2 review; prepare the data for modeling and train your first model. → [Sprint 3](project/sprints.md#sprint-3-first-model)

---

## Session 4 · Classification: logistic regression and metrics

*Casa Central: Wednesday, November 18 · Vitacura: Friday, November 20*

**By the end you can:** train a logistic regression, read a confusion matrix, choose between accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 based on the cost of each error, and adjust the decision threshold.

- [Class 11: Logistic regression and error metrics](classes/Class_11_Logistic_Regression_and_Error_Metrics.ipynb)

**Workshop:** modeling clinic. Each team brings its baseline model and we review it together.

---

## Session 5 · Trees, random forests, and communicating results

*Casa Central: Wednesday, November 25 · Vitacura: Friday, November 27*

**By the end you can:** train decision trees and random forests, control their complexity, read feature importance, compare models with a ROC curve, and build a presentation using the pyramid principle.

- [Class 12b: Decision trees and random forests](classes/Class_12b_Decision_Trees_and_Random_Forest.ipynb)
- [Pyramid presentations in business](classes/Pyramid_Presentations.html){ target="_blank" }

**Workshop:** Sprint 3 review; compare models and structure the final presentation.

---

## Session 6 · Final presentations

*Casa Central: Wednesday, December 2 · Vitacura: Friday, December 4*

Each team presents its project: **10 minutes of presentation and 5 of questions**. The presentation order is published on Monday, November 30. Details are in the [project brief](project/project.md).
