    Machine Learning and Business Applications

    How it works

    01 Five classes, one story. Every class follows Cordillera Mobile, a carrier that wants to know what drives its customers' bills and who is about to cancel. Each class takes that analysis one step further, from Python basics to random forests, and runs in your browser with animated explainers.

    02 One project that grows with the course. From the first class you work in a team on a business case. Each session ends with a workshop where you apply what was just covered.

    03 A weekly advance, every Tuesday. After each session your team hands in the next piece of the project through the team mailbox. You get a grade and comments the following week, so problems surface early.

    04 How it is graded. Weekly advances are 30% of the grade and the final project (notebook + presentation) is 70%. Teams present in the first week of December.
